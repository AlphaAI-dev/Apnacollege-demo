# Competitive Intelligence & Pricing Position (v1.0, 2 Oct 2026)

**Evidence standard:** facts marked ✔ come from public listings found on the research date (sources at the bottom). Items marked ⚠ are hypotheses to verify on each competitor's site and in sales calls before we put them on the website. We never publish a claim about a named competitor that we have not verified.

## 1. Where we win, in one paragraph
Most buyers choose between (a) a **cheap/fast but shallow** option (low-code platforms, offshore hourly agencies) and (b) a **deep but slow and expensive** option (Big 4, 6–12 months). We sit in the gap: **senior-engineer depth, fixed scope, 4–8 weeks, fixed price.**

## 4. Price position: why $28,000

Scope: one ACL deployment. Delivery: 4–8 weeks. **Assumptions are labelled; replace with the client's own numbers on the call.**

| Option | Cost | Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| **AutoScale ACL** | **$28,000 one-off** | Fixed price, owned by client. Ongoing run/support plan: *to be priced (see §6).* |
| Hire an analyst | $75,000/yr salary; **~$94k–105k fully loaded** ⚠ | Loaded cost uses a typical 1.25–1.4× multiplier for benefits/overhead/tools. One person, 40 hrs/week, not 24/7. |
| Offshore agency | $35/hr → **$28,000 buys only 800 hours** (~20 weeks of one engineer) | Rate as given; typical scope creep and rework not included. |
| Vstorm-class boutique | $100–149/hr → **$28,000 ≈ 188–280 hours** ✔ (rates from Clutch) | Hourly, so total depends on scope. |
| Relevance AI Team | $349/mo ≈ $4,188/yr ✔ | Platform fee only; excludes the engineering time to build/maintain and usage overage. |

**Break-even framing:** at $75k salary, $28k equals about **4.5 months** of one analyst's base pay (28 ÷ 75 × 12 = 4.5). If the ACL replaces *half* of an analyst's workload, payback is under 9 months of base salary alone, before loaded cost. This is arithmetic from stated assumptions, not a client result.

## 5. Objection scripts (honest versions)
**"We'll build it with our own team."**
> "Good, you should if you have the people. The risk is time. Agent systems need validation, evals and monitoring, not just prompts. We ship in 4–8 weeks and hand you the code, the docs and the evals. Your team keeps it and runs it."

**"Offshore is $35/hour."**
> "At $35 an hour, $28,000 buys 800 hours, around 20 weeks. We deliver a fixed-scope, validated system in 4–8 weeks for the same money. Ask the agency what happens when output fails validation in production, and who is on the hook."

**"We'll just use Relevance AI / Zapier."**
> "Great for a prototype, and we often build on top of them. If it works for you in production, keep it. If it breaks at volume or on edge cases, that's the point where we come in."

**"Why not a Big 4 firm?"**
> "They are right for transformation programs. We do one thing: get one capability running in weeks, at a fraction of the cost, with senior engineers on the work."

**"Is our data safe?"**
> "Zero data retention is a contract term, not a slide. We'll show you the architecture and put it in the DPA."

Rule: never disparage a competitor by name. Compare on **model, price and timeline**, which the buyer can verify.

## 6. Open decisions
1. **Ongoing cost:** what is the monthly run/support retainer after the $28k? Buyers will ask on call one.
2. **Guarantee:** what do we offer if the 4–8 week deadline slips? (A credit is a strong, cheap trust signal.)
3. **Omnius:** which company did you mean?
4. **Verify ⚠ items** by reading each competitor's site and, if possible, requesting a quote.
